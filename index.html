Card Print Gallery

Paste a list of Magic cards, one a line, and this will look up every English paper printing of each from Scryfall. Quantities and set hints are ignored, so a decklist works as it is.

Give a CubeCobra cube and its list drops in below, ready to edit. Every English paper printing of each card comes from Scryfall.

    Load again after editing the list, or carry on with what was found.

    Resolve each cube's names, then choose what to look at: what they share, or what one has that the other is missing.

    The address bar keeps both cubes, so this comparison can be handed to someone else.
